1 · AI in plain words

"AI" is three different helpers, not one

Generative AI

The writer & reader

The forecaster

⚙️
Automation & agents

The doer

Workflows and AI agents carry out multi-step tasks across your systems (schedule interviews, chase documents, update the HRMS, raise tickets) with human approval at key gates.

Under the hood

How each family works: three short illustrations

A brief look at the mechanism behind each helper. Knowing how each one works makes it easier to judge where it can be trusted and where a human must stay in control.

Mechanism. A large language model is trained on vast amounts of text to estimate the probability of every possible next token, given the text so far. It selects one, appends it and repeats. The result is fluent and plausible, but it is generated, not retrieved from a verified source.

Illustration: the model completing a sentence (click a word to continue)

Implication for HR: Gen AI excels at drafting, summarising and explaining. It can be confidently wrong about facts it was never given, so we supply your approved policies and data, and a person verifies what matters.

Mechanism. A machine-learning model is trained on historical records (for example, past exits) to learn which factors, and in what combination, relate to an outcome. It then scores current records and returns a probability. It is a decision aid, not a verdict, and it can inherit biases present in the history it learned from.

5 · The token economy, made simple

AI runs on a meter. Learn to read it.

Tokens are the unit of cost for generative AI. ML models and automations are priced differently, by compute, data volume or runs, and we will cover that in sizing. Gen AI does not read words. It reads tokens, small chunks of text (roughly ¾ of a word). You pay for tokens going in (your prompt plus any documents) and tokens coming out (the answer). Think of it as a taxi meter: short, direct trips cost less.

6 · Using AI responsibly

People decisions stay with people

Not every HR task should be handed to AI to the same degree. Test your instinct: for each task, how much should AI be trusted?

🟢 Green: AI drafts

Low risk, easy to check. Human gives a quick read before use.

🟡 Amber: AI assists

Useful but consequential. Needs approved sources, careful review and an audit trail.

🔴 Red: Human decides

Rights, livelihoods or sensitive data. AI must not decide, and data must not leave approved tools.

🔒

Protect personal data

Use company-approved AI tools only. Anonymise names, IDs and medical details wherever possible before prompting.

⚖️

Watch for bias

ML models learn from past decisions and can repeat past bias. Anonymise screening data and test outcomes regularly across groups.

🔍

Verify, approve, log

Gen AI can sound sure while being wrong, so check facts. Automations need approval gates and audit trails. Predictions are prompts for conversations.
